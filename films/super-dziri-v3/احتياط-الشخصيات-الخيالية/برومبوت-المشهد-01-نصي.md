# 🎬 برومبوت المشهد 01 — Text-to-Video مباشر (بدون صور)

> **السبب:** المنصة تمنع Image-to-Video للوجوه الحقيقية.
> **الحل:** نعطيها **وصف نصي كامل** → هي تولّد الشخصية والفيديو من الصفر.
> **الاستعمال:** في Dreamina أو أي أداة → اختر **Text to Video** → الصق هذا البرومبوت كاملاً.

---

## 📝 البرومبوت الكامل (انسخ هذا كله والصقه في Text to Video)

```
CREATE A 10-SECOND PHOTOREALISTIC CINEMATIC FILM SHOT, 16:9, from scratch (no reference image).

STYLE: Realistic live-action Algerian cinema, natural color grading, subtle film grain,
35mm cinematic lens, shallow depth of field. The character looks like a normal everyday
Algerian young man — NOT a celebrity, NOT a known face.

CHARACTER:
A young Algerian man named Anis Benyoucef, 25 years old, 178 cm, slim athletic build,
short black wavy hair, thin dark eyebrows, dark brown eyes, light stubble beard on the
jawline, medium olive-tan skin. He wears a light grey t-shirt, blue jeans and dark
sneakers, carrying a small plastic bag of vegetables. Completely ordinary appearance,
like a random person on the street.

ENVIRONMENT:
A busy authentic Algerian neighborhood vegetable market in the late afternoon.
Colorful vegetable stalls, crates of tomatoes and peppers, hanging scales, old signs,
modest architecture, ordinary shoppers and vendors moving naturally. Warm golden
afternoon sunlight, long soft shadows, natural dust in the air.

CAMERA:
Starts with a medium-wide rear three-quarter shot of Anis walking into the market,
then smoothly tracks forward with him as he approaches a vegetable stall, ending on a
medium side shot. Handheld but controlled, realistic camera movement, no sudden jumps.

TIMELINE:
0-2 seconds: Anis walks into the market entrance at a normal human pace, arms swinging
naturally, the bag moving with his steps.
2-5 seconds: He walks forward through the market toward the vegetable stalls, looking
around naturally at the produce.
5-8 seconds: He slows down as he reaches a tomato stall, his body turns slightly toward
the vendor.
8-10 seconds: He stops beside the stall and reaches his right hand toward a pile of
tomatoes, settling into a natural standing position.

DIALOGUE:
Anis says naturally in Algerian Darija: «ربي يجيب الخير اليوم.»
(Translation: "May God bring goodness today.")
He speaks while walking, realistic lip-sync, clear and calm voice. Do not add any other
dialogue.

AUDIO:
Natural Algerian market ambience — vendors calling, people chatting, footsteps on
concrete, distant car horns. The dialogue must be clear and audible above the ambience.
No music.

CHARACTER CONSISTENCY:
Keep the same face, hairstyle, grey t-shirt, blue jeans and body proportions throughout
the entire 10 seconds. The character must not change identity or appearance.

ENDING:
The final frame shows Anis standing naturally at the tomato stall, right hand near the
tomatoes — a clean, stable frame ready to be the starting point of the next scene.

STRICT RULES:
No teleportation. No morphing. No sudden location change. No costume change.
No identity drift. No text on screen. No subtitles. No watermark. No logo.
```

---

## 🎯 كيفاش تستعمل (خطوات):

1. افتح **Dreamina** (أو أي أداة فيديو)
2. اختر **"Text to Video"** (إنشاء من النص) — **مش** Image to Video
3. الصق البرومبوت أعلاه كاملاً
4. اختر المقاس **16:9**
5. ولّد — المنصة تولّد الشخصية + الفيديو **من النص فقط** (بلا حظر لأن ما رفعتش صورة)

---

## ⚠️ ملاحظات مهمة:

| النقطة | التوضيح |
|---|---|
| ✅ **لماذا ينجح؟** | ما فيش صورة مرفوعة → ما فيش وجه "حقيقي" يتعرف عليه → بلا حظر |
| ⚠️ **الاستمرارية** | كل مشهد يوصف بنفس الشخصية → المنصة تولّدها متشابهة (مش مثالية لكن مقبولة) |
| 💡 **الأفضل** | استعمل نفس "وصف الشخصية" في كل المشاهد الـ24 → الاستمرارية أفضل |

---

## 📁 الخطوة الجاية:

**قلّي "كمل"** → نكتب **كل المشاهد الـ24 بنفس الطريقة** (Text-to-Video مباشر) في ملف واحد كبير:
- كل مشهد: وصف كامل (شخصية + مكان + حركة + حوار + صوت + كاميرا)
- جاهز للنسخ والصق مباشرة

**جرب هذا البرومبوت الأول في المنصة** وقلّي واش صار 🎬
