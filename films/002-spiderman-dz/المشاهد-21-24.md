## 🎬 المشهد 21 — "يوقف سرقة سيارة" (6 ثواني) 🚗

![مشهد 21](المشاهد/مشهد-21-سرقة-السيارة.png)

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic action scene, 6 seconds, Algerian street at dusk, dramatic warm light.

CHARACTER: Spider-Man, complete red and blue suit with black web pattern, full mask
covering his ENTIRE face with large white eyes (no face visible), slim athletic build.

ENVIRONMENT: Algerian street with low buildings, street lamps glowing, a white car.

CAMERA: Dynamic low-angle heroic shot.

ACTION WITH TIMING:
- 0-2 seconds: A thief with a crowbar tries to break into a white car, Spider-Man
  lands silently behind him.
- 2-4 seconds: The thief turns and sees Spider-Man, drops the crowbar in shock,
  hands up.
- 4-6 seconds: Spider-Man points to the car, then to the street, gesturing "go
  walk" — the thief runs away, Spider-Man shakes his head amused.

DIALOGUE (Algerian Darija, text on screen):
"سرقة سيارة في وضح النهار؟! شويا من الحشمة!
(للسارق) هاك كاين الشرطة في الشارع التالي... وإلا تفضل تبقى هنا ونصورك
سيلفي ونحطها في الإنستغرام؟! ههه، يلا امشي!"
Thief (يهرب): "ما نرجعش! ما نرجعش!"

SOUND: Dusk ambience, a dramatic landing thud, comedic chase footsteps.
```

## 🎬 المشهد 22 — "شاي مع خالتي زهرة" (6 ثواني) 🍵

![مشهد 22](المشاهد/مشهد-22-شاي-زهرة.png)

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic comedic heartwarming scene, 6 seconds, inside a modest Algerian home, warm afternoon light.

CHARACTERS:
- Spider-Man: complete red and blue suit, full mask with large white eyes, sitting
  at a small table holding a glass of mint tea.
- Aunt Zahra: elderly Algerian woman, 72 years old, grey hair in a bun under a
  white haik head covering, dark blue traditional dress, smiling warmly.

ENVIRONMENT: Cozy modest Algerian living room with traditional decor, small table
with tea and pastries.

CAMERA: Medium shot of the two at the table.

ACTION WITH TIMING:
- 0-2 seconds: Spider-Man sits at the table holding the mint tea, looking at it
  a bit awkwardly (drinking with a mask on is funny).
- 2-4 seconds: Aunt Zahra pushes the plate of pastries toward him, insisting he eat.
- 4-6 seconds: Spider-Man lifts the bottom edge of his mask slightly to sip the
  tea, Zahra laughs warmly, he gives a thumbs up.

DIALOGUE (Algerian Darija, text on screen):
Zahra: "اشرب يا ولدي، الشاي يبرد! وكل كعبة!"
Spider-Man (يرفع حافة القناع بشوية): "شكرا خالتي... ولكن، شوفيني كياش
نشرب بالقناع هذا؟ ههه!"
Zahra: "حتى مع القناع، نعرفك... إنت اللي رجعتلي حقيبتي. يجعلها في ميزان
حسناتك!"
Spider-Man: "خالتي، أنا غير خدمت واجبي. الحومة تاعنا، وكيما نقولو:
'إيد وحدة ما تصفقش.'"

SOUND: Tea pouring, warm laughter, cozy home ambience, a cat purring.
```

## 🎬 المشهد 23 — "تصالح مع قويدر" (6 ثواني) 🤝

![مشهد 23](المشاهد/مشهد-23-تصالح-قويدر.png)

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic cinematic scene, 6 seconds, Algerian street at golden hour, warm respectful mood.

CHARACTERS:
- Spider-Man: complete red and blue suit, full mask with large white eyes.
- Officer Kouider: 40 years old, grey-flecked black hair, thick moustache, dark
  blue Algerian police uniform with cap, a small respectful smile.

ENVIRONMENT: Low Algerian buildings, warm golden hour light.

CAMERA: Medium shot of the two shaking hands.

ACTION WITH TIMING:
- 0-2 seconds: Kouider extends his hand to Spider-Man, looking at him with respect.
- 2-4 seconds: Spider-Man shakes his hand firmly, both nodding.
- 4-6 seconds: Kouider tips his cap and points to the sky, as if saying "keep
  watching over the neighborhood," Spider-Man returns a thumbs up and turns to leave.

DIALOGUE (Algerian Darija, text on screen):
Kouider: "أول مرة نحب نقولها... شكرا. الحومة صارت أهدى بياك.
وخا ما نعرفش وجهك... نعرف إنك من هنا."
Spider-Man: "شكرا خويا قويدر. أنا وأنا واحد منكم... غير عندي قناع.
(يضحك) واذا احتجت حاجة، ارمي في السماء ونبان."
Kouider: "نعتمد عليك يا 'الرجل العنكبوت'. وبرك، حافظ على نفسك."
Spider-Man: "وعد. ونشوفك في الخدمة."

SOUND: Golden hour ambience, a firm handshake, respectful music, then a web swish.
```

## 🎬 المشهد 24 — "النهاية" (6 ثواني) 🌅

![مشهد 24](المشاهد/مشهد-24-النهاية.png)

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic cinematic final scene, 6 seconds, Algerian rooftop at sunset, emotional peaceful mood.

CHARACTER: Spider-Man, complete red and blue suit, full mask with large white eyes,
sitting on the edge of a low rooftop, feet dangling over the edge.

ENVIRONMENT: Low Algerian buildings with satellite dishes, minarets and laundry on
balconies, deep orange and purple sunset sky, city lights beginning to glow below.

CAMERA: Wide cinematic shot from behind-side, showing him and the city view.

ACTION WITH TIMING:
- 0-2 seconds: Spider-Man sits on the rooftop edge, looking calmly over the city.
- 2-4 seconds: He looks down at the neighborhood below — children playing, people
  walking, lights turning on — a peaceful satisfied posture.
- 4-6 seconds: He looks up at the sky, nods to himself, then a slow zoom out —
  freeze frame on him against the sunset (end card with title).

DIALOGUE (Algerian Darija, text on screen — inner voice):
"في نيويورك، سبايدرمان يطير بين الأبراج. هنا في الجزائر...
نطير بين القلوب. مافيهش أبراج، مافيهش مشاكل؟! لا، مليحة فيها.
ولكن، البطل الحقيقي ماشي اللي يطير... البطل اللي يوقف مع الناس في
الطابور، في البلدية، في السوق. (يبتسم تحت القناع) يلا، نرجع نخدم.
غدوة نهار آخر. لأن الحومة تاعنا، تستاهل."

SOUND: Emotional cinematic music, city ambience at dusk, wind, a gentle web
swish, then the end card music.
```
