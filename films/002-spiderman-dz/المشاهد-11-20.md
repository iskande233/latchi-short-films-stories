## 🎬 المشهد 11 — "الحي يتكلم" (6 ثواني) 🗣️

![مشهد 11](المشاهد/مشهد-11-الحي-يتكلم.png)

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic cinematic scene, 6 seconds, Algerian neighborhood street, sunny morning.

CHARACTER: Anis, a young Algerian man, 25 years old, short black wavy hair, light
stubble on jawline, medium olive-tan skin, wearing a light grey t-shirt and blue
jeans (completely normal, NO mask, nobody knows his secret).

ENVIRONMENT: Low Algerian buildings, sidewalk with a group of neighbors.

CAMERA: Medium shot from behind the neighbors, showing Anis walking and them
whispering.

ACTION WITH TIMING:
- 0-2 seconds: Anis walks down the street carrying a bag of bread, looking normal.
- 2-4 seconds: A group of neighbors on the sidewalk notice him and start
  whispering to each other, pointing slightly.
- 4-6 seconds: Anis notices them looking at him, gives a confused friendly smile
  and keeps walking.

DIALOGUE (Algerian Darija, text on screen — neighbors whispering):
Neighbor 1: "هذا هو اللي يهز في الحومة؟"
Neighbor 2: "آه، نسمعو عليه... الرجل العنكبوت!"
Neighbor 1: "لا، هذا أنيس... ولد الحومة، عادي."
Anis (لنفسه): "واش يهمسو عليّ؟ المهم، ياك ما عرفونيش."

SOUND: Morning street ambience, birds, low whispers, then Anis's light footsteps.
```

```
Photorealistic comedic scene, 6 seconds, inside an Algerian taxi, warm daylight.

CHARACTERS:
- Anis: young Algerian man, 25 years old, short black wavy hair, light stubble,
  olive-tan skin, light grey t-shirt (normal clothes, NO mask).

Anis (متفاجئ): "واتساب؟! لا لا خويا... برك صورة وحدة!"

SOUND: Taxi engine, laughter, phone camera shutter click.

## 🎬 المشهد 13 — "طابور المخبزة" (6 ثواني) 🥖

![مشهد 13](المشاهد/مشهد-13-طابور-المخبزة.png)

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic comedic scene, 6 seconds, Algerian street bakery (khobza), warm morning light.

CHARACTER: Anis, young Algerian man, 25 years old, short black wavy hair, light
stubble, olive-tan skin, light grey t-shirt, blue jeans (normal clothes, NO mask).

ENVIRONMENT: Simple street bakery with a queue of people, an elderly woman with
a baguette in front of him.

CAMERA: Medium shot from behind Anis, showing the queue ahead.

ACTION WITH TIMING:
- 0-2 seconds: Anis stands in the bread queue, looking at the long line ahead
  with an impatient funny expression.
- 2-4 seconds: He counts the people in front of him with his fingers, sighing.
- 4-6 seconds: The elderly woman in front takes her bread and leaves, Anis steps
  forward with a hopeful look.

DIALOGUE (Algerian Darija, text on screen):
"يا خويا، الطابور هذا؟! نهار ولا عام؟! أنا اللي نقدر نطير فوقهم... ولكن لا،
البطل الحقيقي يوقف في الطابور كيما الناس. المهم، الخبز سخون."
(Anis looks at his small change and sighs)
"غير نقدر نشري غير نصيب... والأرزاق على الله."

SOUND: Bakery ambience, people chatting, bread being taken out of the oven.
```

## 🎬 المشهد 14 — "المنتجات المقلدة" (6 ثواني) 👟

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic scene, 6 seconds, Algerian market, warm daylight.

CHARACTER: Anis, young Algerian man, 25 years old, short black wavy hair, light
stubble, olive-tan skin, light grey t-shirt, blue jeans (normal clothes, NO mask).

ENVIRONMENT: Market stall with counterfeit products (fake branded sneakers and
watches) displayed on a table, a thin sly seller.

CAMERA: Medium shot over Anis's shoulder looking at the stall.

ACTION WITH TIMING:

- 2-4 seconds: He notices the poor stitching and the fake logo, shaking his head.
- 4-6 seconds: He looks at the seller with a knowing look, the seller smiles
  nervously, Anis puts the shoe down and walks away determined.

DIALOGUE (Algerian Darija, text on screen — Anis thinking):
"هادي؟! ماركة عالمية بـ 1500 دج؟! هذا نصاب، يبيع المقلد للناس.
المشكلة، الناس ما يعرفوش. ولكن... أنا نعرف.
(ينظر للساعة) واش يلزم نعمل؟ نرجع الليلة."

SOUND: Market noise, chatter of sellers, a fake watch ticking sound.
```

## 🎬 المشهد 17 — "مساعدة قويدر" (6 ثواني) 👮

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic scene, 6 seconds, Algerian street at dusk, warm light.

CHARACTERS:
- Spider-Man: complete red and blue suit, full mask, large white eyes.
- Officer Kouider: 40 years old, grey-flecked black hair, thick moustache, dark
  blue Algerian police uniform with cap.

ENVIRONMENT: Low Algerian buildings, street at dusk.

CAMERA: Medium shot of the two facing each other.

- 0-2 seconds: Spider-Man hands a captured small criminal by the arm to Officer

- 2-4 seconds: Kouider looks at Spider-Man with a mix of suspicion and respect,

- 4-6 seconds: Kouider tips his cap slightly in a thank-you gesture, Spider-Man
  nods back.

DIALOGUE (Algerian Darija, text on screen):
Kouider: "راك تحب تقوم بدوري؟!"
Spider-Man: "خويا قويدر، أنا ما نحبش نحل محلك... غير نساعد. هذا اللي كان
يسرق في الحومة، خذيوه."
Kouider: "يعني... أنت ماشي شرير؟"
Spider-Man (يضحك): "لا خويا، أنا ولد هذي الحومة. غير القناع غيّرني."
Kouider: "نشوفك... نراقبك."

SOUND: Dusk ambience, police radio static, firm but respectful voices.
