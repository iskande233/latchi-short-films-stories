# 🕷️ سبايدرمان ديالنا — المشاهد (1-5) برومبوتات كاملة

> 📌 **النظام:** كل مشهد = برومبوت واحد كامل (فيه كل شيء: الوصف + الشخصيات + الحركة + الكلام بالدارجة + الصوت + الكاميرا).
> **المدة:** كل مشهد 6 ثواني.

---

## 🧑🎨 الشخصيات (مرجع ثابت — يبقى نفسهم في كل المشاهد)

### 🕷️ أنيس / سبايدرمان
```
Photorealistic young Algerian man, Anis, 25 years old, 178 cm tall, slim athletic
build, short black wavy hair, thin dark eyebrows, expressive dark brown eyes,
light stubble beard covering the jawline, medium olive-tan skin, small scar on
his left eyebrow. As Spider-Man he wears the classic red-and-blue suit with black
web pattern, large white expressive eyes on the mask. Same identity in every scene.
```

### 👵 خالتي زهرة
```
Photorealistic elderly Algerian woman, Aunt Zahra, 72 years old, short and slightly
hunched, deep wrinkles, warm brown eyes, grey hair in a bun under a traditional
white haik head covering, dark blue traditional karakou-style dress with gold
embroidery, worn brown leather handbag. Same appearance in every scene.
```

### 🧑 حرامي السوق
```
Photorealistic thin Algerian man, market thief, 30 years old, thin build, shifty
narrow eyes, short black messy hair, thin goatee, light brown skin, dark grey
hooded jacket, black jeans, small black bag. Same appearance in every scene.
```

## 🎬 المشهد 1 — "اللدغة في السوق" (6 ثواني)

![مشهد 1](المشاهد/مشهد-01-اللدغة.png)

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic cinematic scene, 6 seconds, Algerian souk market, warm afternoon light.

CHARACTER: Anis, a young Algerian man, 25 years old, short black wavy hair, light
stubble beard on jawline, medium olive-tan skin, wearing a light grey t-shirt and
blue jeans, holding a bag of tomatoes at a vegetable stall.

ENVIRONMENT: A busy Algerian vegetable market, colorful vegetable stalls, blurred
shoppers moving around, warm afternoon sunlight.

CAMERA: Medium close-up on Anis's hand and face.

ACTION WITH TIMING:
- 0-2 seconds: Anis picks tomatoes at the market stall, looking at the vegetables.
- 2-4 seconds: A small spider suddenly bites his hand, he winces and looks down.
- 4-6 seconds: He looks at the small bite on his hand with a surprised confused expression.

DIALOGUE (Algerian Arabic, text on screen): "آي! واش هادي؟! عنكبوت؟ في السوق؟!"

SOUND: Market ambience, people talking, then a sudden "ouch" sound.
```

## 🎬 المشهد 2 — "محاولة الطيران الفاشلة" (6 ثواني)

![مشهد 2](المشاهد/مشهد-02-محاولة-الطيران.png)

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic comedic cinematic scene, 6 seconds, Algerian low-rise neighborhood, warm afternoon light.

CHARACTER: Spider-Man (Anis in the classic red-and-blue suit with black web pattern,
large white eyes on the mask, slim athletic build).

ENVIRONMENT: Rooftop of a low 3-story Algerian building, surrounding buildings are
all low (2-4 stories), satellite dishes, laundry hanging on balconies, minarets in
the distance, blue sky.

CAMERA: Wide shot showing Spider-Man on the rooftop and the low city around him.

ACTION WITH TIMING:
- 0-2 seconds: Spider-Man raises his hand to shoot a web, confident pose.
- 2-4 seconds: He shoots a web, but it only reaches a small balcony railing nearby,
  he looks at his hand in comedic disbelief.
- 4-6 seconds: He sags awkwardly mid-air, holding the web, looking down at the
  short distance to the ground with a defeated funny expression.

DIALOGUE (Algerian Arabic, text on screen): "هذا؟! ها هو سطيح من 3 طوابق؟! وين الأبراج في هذي البلاد؟!"

SOUND: Web shooting sound, comedic sad trombone, pigeon cooing.
```

## 🎬 المشهد 3 — "سرقة الحقيبة" (6 ثواني)

![مشهد 3](المشاهد/مشهد-03-سرقة-الحقيبة.png)

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic cinematic action scene, 6 seconds, Algerian street market, warm daylight.

CHARACTERS:
- Market thief: thin young Algerian man, 30 years old, short messy black hair,
  thin goatee, dark grey hooded jacket, black jeans.
- Aunt Zahra: elderly Algerian woman, 72 years old, grey hair in a bun under a
  white haik head covering, dark blue traditional dress, brown leather handbag.

ENVIRONMENT: Busy Algerian street market with vegetable and goods stalls, shocked
shoppers around.

CAMERA: Dynamic medium shot capturing the snatch moment.

ACTION WITH TIMING:
- 0-2 seconds: The thief sneaks behind Aunt Zahra, reaches for her handbag.
- 2-4 seconds: He snatches the brown handbag from her arm and starts running away.
- 4-6 seconds: Aunt Zahra cries out, raising her hands in shock, shoppers turn around.

DIALOGUE (Algerian Arabic, text on screen): "أي يا ولدي! الحرامي! الحقو! سرقلي حقيبتي!"

SOUND: Market noise suddenly stops, a gasp, quick footsteps.
```

## 🎬 المشهد 4 — "الإمساك بالحرامي" (6 ثواني)

![مشهد 4](المشاهد/مشهد-04-الإمساك-بالحرامي.png)

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic cinematic action scene, 6 seconds, narrow Algerian alley, warm afternoon light.

CHARACTERS:
- Spider-Man: red and blue suit with black web pattern, white eyes on mask.
- Market thief: thin young man, 30 years old, messy black hair, goatee, dark grey
  hooded jacket, scared expression.

ENVIRONMENT: Narrow stone-walled alley in an old Algerian neighborhood.

CAMERA: Dramatic low-angle medium shot.

ACTION WITH TIMING:
- 0-2 seconds: Spider-Man holds the thief by the collar against the alley wall.
- 2-4 seconds: The thief struggles scared, Spider-Man shakes his head disapprovingly.
- 4-6 seconds: Spider-Man points down at the brown handbag on the ground, then at
  the thief with a "shame on you" gesture.

DIALOGUE (Algerian Arabic, text on screen): "تسرق حقيبة وحدة عجوز؟! عيب عليك! هذي حقيبة خالتي زهرة!"

SOUND: Dramatic tension music, a scuffle, then a firm tone.
```

## 🎬 المشهد 5 — "انقطاع الكهرباء" (6 ثواني)

![مشهد 5](المشاهد/مشهد-05-انقطاع-الكهرباء.png)

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic cinematic heroic scene, 6 seconds, Algerian neighborhood at dusk, orange sky.

CHARACTER: Spider-Man (red and blue suit, white eyes on mask) climbing an electricity pole.

ENVIRONMENT: Low Algerian apartment building, residents on balconies watching,
one balcony with working warm lights, the rest dark, damaged transformer box
sparking, orange dusk sky.

CAMERA: Dramatic low-angle wide shot showing Spider-Man high on the pole.

ACTION WITH TIMING:
- 0-2 seconds: Spider-Man climbs the electricity pole, sparks flying from the
  damaged transformer.
- 2-4 seconds: He uses his web to fix and re-connect the transformer wires, sparks stop.
- 4-6 seconds: The building lights turn on one by one, residents on balconies cheer
  with raised hands.

DIALOGUE (Algerian Arabic, text on screen): "ها هي الضو رجعت! أنيس، والله أنت بطل!" (الناس يصفقون)

SOUND: Electrical crackle, then a "click" and joyful cheering from the neighborhood.
```
