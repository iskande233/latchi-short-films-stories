> 📌 **القاعدة المطبقة:** الهوية السرية — أنيس عادي بدون قناع، سبايدرمان ببدلة كاملة والوجه مخفي.

## 🎬 المشهد 6 — "التاكسي" (6 ثواني) 🚖

![مشهد 6](المشاهد/مشهد-06-التاكسي.png)

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic cinematic scene, 6 seconds, inside an Algerian taxi, warm daylight.

CHARACTERS:
- Anis: young Algerian man, 25 years old, short black wavy hair, light stubble
  on jawline, medium olive-tan skin, wearing a light grey t-shirt and blue jeans
  (completely normal casual clothes, NO mask, NO costume, nobody knows his identity).
- Si Tahar: Algerian taxi driver, 50 years old, grey moustache, beige shirt,
  friendly face, driving.

ENVIRONMENT: Inside a white and yellow Algerian taxi, city street visible through
the windows, warm daylight.

CAMERA: Medium shot from the back seat area, showing both Anis and the driver.

ACTION WITH TIMING:
- 0-2 seconds: Anis sits in the back seat, relaxed, looking out the window.
- 2-4 seconds: The driver looks back and asks him something with a friendly smile.
- 4-6 seconds: Anis answers casually, then looks out the window with a small
  knowing smile (as if he has a secret).

DIALOGUE (Algerian Darija, text on screen):
Driver: "وين رايح يا ولدي؟ علاش كسول هكاك؟"
Anis: "خويا، راني رايح نخدم... وخا الخدمة ما تبانش، غير توقّع المفاجآت."
Driver: "ياك تتكلم على الفلوس؟"
Anis (يبتسم): "لا خويا، على شيء ما تتصوّروش..."

SOUND: Taxi engine, city traffic, casual friendly tones, then a mysterious soft
laugh from Anis.
```

## 🎬 المشهد 7 — "التحول" (6 ثواني) 🎒

![مشهد 7](المشاهد/مشهد-07-التحول.png)

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic cinematic scene, 6 seconds, hidden narrow Algerian alley, dramatic
warm light, secretive mood.

CHARACTER: Anis, young Algerian man, 25 years old, short black wavy hair, light
stubble, olive-tan skin, wearing a light grey t-shirt and blue jeans, holding an
open black sports bag.

ENVIRONMENT: Empty hidden narrow alley with old stone walls, dramatic shafts of
warm light.

CAMERA: Medium shot, slightly low angle for drama.

ACTION WITH TIMING:
- 0-2 seconds: Anis looks around to make sure nobody is watching, then opens his
  black sports bag.
- 2-4 seconds: He pulls out the red and blue Spider-Man suit from the bag, the
  fabric catching the light.
- 4-6 seconds: He holds the suit up, a determined look on his face, ready to
  change (cut before he actually wears it — the suit covers his face only in
  the next action scene).

DIALOGUE (Algerian Darija, text on screen, whispered to himself):
"يا الله... كي نلبس هادي، نولي 'سبايدرمان'... وخا ما يكونوش عارفين مين أنا،
المهم الناس تكون مرتاحة. يلا، نبداو الخدمة."

SOUND: Dramatic suspense music building up, zipper sound of the bag, fabric
rustling.
```

## 🎬 المشهد 8 — "سبايدرمان يظهر للناس" (6 ثواني) 💪

![مشهد 8](المشاهد/مشهد-08-الهجوم.png)

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic cinematic scene, 6 seconds, Algerian street at dusk, warm orange
light, festive heroic mood.

CHARACTER: Spider-Man, wearing the complete red and blue suit with black web
pattern, full mask covering his ENTIRE face — only large white expressive eyes
visible, NO skin or face shown at all. He stands on a low wall in a heroic
confident pose, one hand raised waving, the stolen brown handbag returned on a
table beside him.

ENVIRONMENT: Algerian street with low buildings, residents watching from
balconies, excited children in the street, warm dusk sky.

CAMERA: Low-angle wide shot making Spider-Man look heroic.

ACTION WITH TIMING:
- 0-2 seconds: Spider-Man lands/stands on the low wall, raising his hand in a
  heroic greeting.
- 2-4 seconds: He points to the returned handbag, then to himself with a thumbs
  up, residents clap.
- 4-6 seconds: He does a small dramatic bow, children cheer, he waves goodbye
  and prepares to leave.

DIALOGUE (Algerian Darija, text on screen):
"سلام عليكم! أنا سبايدرمان... وخا ما تعرفوش وجهي، المهم تعرفوا إني هنا نحمي
الحومة تاعنا. خالتي زهرة، الحقيبة تاعك رجعات، وعيشة ماشي مسروقة!"

SOUND: Cheering crowd, children laughing, heroic upbeat music, then a web
shooting "swish" as he leaves.
```

## 🎬 المشهد 9 — "البلدية" (6 ثواني) 🏛️

![مشهد 9](المشاهد/مشهد-09-البلدية.png)

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic cinematic scene, 6 seconds, inside an Algerian municipal office
(baladiya), daylight through windows.

CHARACTERS:
- Anis: young Algerian man, 25 years old, short black wavy hair, light stubble,
  olive-tan skin, light grey t-shirt and blue jeans (completely normal, NO mask).
- Aunt Zahra: elderly Algerian woman, 72 years old, grey hair in a bun under a
  white haik head covering, dark blue traditional dress, holding papers.

ENVIRONMENT: Algerian baladiya office interior, counter with employee behind a
window, other people waiting, official paperwork atmosphere.

CAMERA: Medium shot of the two at the counter.

ACTION WITH TIMING:
- 0-2 seconds: Zahra looks confused at her papers, shaking her head.
- 2-4 seconds: Anis leans in, pointing at the paper and explaining patiently.
- 4-6 seconds: Zahra smiles gratefully, Anis nods kindly, they move to the
  counter together.

DIALOGUE (Algerian Darija, text on screen):
Zahra: "يا ولدي، هذي الوثائق نهار ولا عام ما كملتهمش... البيروقراطية هادي
ماشي شغلة."
Anis: "لا تخافي يا خالتي، أنا معاك. هاك توقيعي هنا، وتوقيعك هنا، وروحو نكملو
مع بعض. فين رانا فين؟! نكملو شغلتنا وما نخليوكش."
Zahra (تضحك): "يعيشك يا ولدي، واش كنا نعملو بلايك؟"

SOUND: Office ambience, papers shuffling, stamp sounds, warm kind voices.
```

## 🎬 المشهد 10 — "النهاية" (6 ثواني) 🌅

![مشهد 10](المشاهد/مشهد-10-النهاية.png)

**البرومبوت الكامل:**
```
Photorealistic cinematic final scene, 6 seconds, Algerian rooftop at sunset,
emotional peaceful mood.

CHARACTER: Spider-Man, complete red and blue suit, full mask covering his face
with large white eyes, sitting on the edge of a low rooftop, feet dangling over
the edge, looking over the city.

ENVIRONMENT: Low Algerian buildings with satellite dishes and minarets, laundry
on balconies, orange and purple sunset sky, city lights beginning to glow.

CAMERA: Wide cinematic shot from behind-side, showing him and the city view.

ACTION WITH TIMING:
- 0-2 seconds: Spider-Man sits on the rooftop edge, looking over the city calmly.
- 2-4 seconds: He looks down at his hands, then back at the city, a peaceful
  moment (even with the mask, his posture shows a smile).
- 4-6 seconds: He looks up at the sky, nods to himself, then a slow zoom out —
  freeze frame on him against the sunset (end card).

DIALOGUE (Algerian Darija, text on screen — inner thought, voiceover):
"الناس يعرفوني باسم سبايدرمان، ولكن... في الحقيقة أنا أنيس، ولد الحومة.
حتى بلا أبراج، وبلا ضجيج نيويورك... نقدر نخدم في بلادي. لأن البطل الحقيقي،
ماشي اللي يطير... البطل اللي يقف مع الناس. والجزائر، تستاهل بطلها.
(يبتسم تحت القناع) يلا، نرجع نخدم. غدوة نهار آخر."

SOUND: Emotional cinematic music, city ambience at dusk, a gentle wind, then
a soft web "swish" as the end card appears.
```
